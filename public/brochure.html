Welcome

A home built on compassion

Luna Cottage Adult Family Home is a licensed, RN-owned and managed care home located in a peaceful residential neighborhood in South Everett. We provide a warm, safe, and comfortable environment for up to six residents, offering personalized care tailored to each individual's needs, preferences, and abilities.

Our home is designed to feel like family, where residents are treated with dignity, respect, and compassion while receiving the support they need to live comfortably and safely. Small-setting senior care is a wonderful alternative to a nursing home or large assisted living facility.

“Our mission is to provide high-quality, compassionate, and individualized care in a home-like setting where every resident feels valued, safe, and supported.”

Our philosophy of care

Our philosophy is centered on person-centered care. We believe each resident is unique and deserves care that respects their history, preferences, and current abilities, in a nurturing environment that supports physical, emotional, and social well-being.
